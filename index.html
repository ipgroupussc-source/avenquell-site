Questions

What people ask before they start.

Where should I start?

Most engagements start with a diagnostic. If the question is about visuals and direction, that's the Visual Diagnosis. If ad spend is running but results aren't following, that's the Ad Diagnostic. If there's no data history yet, that's Buyer Research.

Do you need access to my ad accounts?

No. Diagnostics work from exports and screenshots you send us. The report states plainly which findings cannot be verified without live access, so you know exactly what we read and what we didn't.

How fast is a diagnostic?

Three business days for the Visual Diagnosis and five for the Ad Diagnostic — counted from the point we have everything we need from you, not from the day you pay.

Is there a long-term contract?

No annual contract and no prepayment for the year. The Monthly Content runs on a two-month minimum; other monthly services run on three months. Either ends with 30 days notice, by email.

What if the diagnostic doesn't find anything?

On the Ad Diagnostic we name the cause or refund the fee. The things we can't verify without account access are listed before you pay, and they're excluded from that guarantee — so the promise covers what we can actually establish.

Do you use AI?

Yes, as a production tool. Direction is decided by people before anything is produced, and every asset is reviewed by a person against the approved direction before it ships.

Can I see your work?

What's published are concept studies, labelled as speculative. They show how the method works — direction, production logic, asset systems — rather than claiming campaigns we didn't run.
