My ads are running but sales aren't following. Where do I look first?

Look at what you are counting before you look at the creative. In most accounts that say this, something is being counted as a conversion that is not a sale, and every decision made downstream of that number was made on a false reading.

Three checks you can run yourself this week

One: read what each conversion event actually counts. Open your conversion settings and go through them one at a time. A button click, an add to cart, a page view and a purchase all arrive in the report under the same word. Only one of them is money. If a campaign is being optimised toward a button click, the platform will faithfully find you people who click buttons.

Two: pull the last sixty to ninety days at the creative level, not the campaign level. Campaign averages hide the thing you need to see. In most accounts, one creative carries the spend and two or three are quietly eating the rest. You are looking for which specific asset the money went to, and what it did.

Three: put the ad and the landing page side by side and read them as a stranger. Does the first screen of the page answer the promise the ad just made, in the same words? A click is a question. Pages that answer a different question lose people who were already sold.

What those checks cannot tell you

Whether your pixel fires correctly on live traffic. How tracking behaves when someone crosses from one domain to another. What settings were changed six weeks ago and by whom. Those need exports read against each other, and they are where a surprising number of these cases end.

If you want someone else to read it

That is the Ad Diagnostic: $3,500, five business days, worked entirely from exports and screenshots you send — campaign exports for sixty to ninety days, a GA4 export for the same period, your average order value and rough margin, and what your business counts as a conversion. No logins and no account access.

It ends with the cause named, a ranked order to fix things in, and three to five ad concepts built around the diagnosis. If the required material was complete and the data did not support a named cause, you are refunded.

One condition worth saying plainly: it is not a fit if your ads have been running less than two or three months. There is not enough history in the account to read, and a diagnosis built on two weeks of data is a guess with an invoice attached.
