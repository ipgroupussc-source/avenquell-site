Answers

Questions people ask before they hire anyone.

Plain answers to the things brands ask us, including the ones that argue against buying from us. Each page answers in the first two sentences, then shows the working.

Why pay for a visual review when other studios run audits for free?

Because they are not the same purchase. A free audit is the first half of a sales conversation and ends in a proposal; a paid one ends in a written document you can hand to anyone and act on without us — and if the material did not support a named cause, you do not pay for it.

Read the answer →

How do I know the images won't look AI-generated?

What makes an image read as AI is rarely the tool — it is the default look a model falls back on when nobody has decided what the picture is for. You can check that before you buy: open the work at full size and look at the product details and the light.

Read the answer →

My ads are running but sales aren't following. Where do I look first?

Look at what you are counting before you look at the creative. In most accounts that say this, something is being counted as a conversion that is not a sale, and every decision made downstream of that number was made on a false reading.
