Why pay for a visual review when other studios run audits for free?

Because they are not the same purchase. A free audit is the first half of a sales conversation and ends in a proposal; a paid one ends in a written document you can hand to anyone and act on without us — and if the material did not support a named cause, you do not pay for it.

What a free audit is for

It qualifies you. That is not an insult to it — a good one is genuinely useful, and if a studio is offering you twenty minutes of attention on your account, take it. You will learn what an experienced person notices quickly, which is worth having. It ends where the proposal begins, because that is the job it was built to do.

The question that separates the two

Not “which one goes deeper”. You cannot check that before you buy, so arguing about it is noise. The question is what is in your hands at the end, and whether you could act on it with a different studio, a freelancer, or your own team.

If what you hold at the end is a slide deck of observations and a price, you bought a sales conversation. If it is a document that names one cause, ranks what to fix first, and comes with concepts you can brief someone else from, you bought work.

What this one leaves behind

  • A written report, signed, naming the cause and ranking what to fix in what order.
  • In the ads version, three to five ad concepts built around the diagnosis, so the report ends with something to run rather than something to read.
  • A refund if the required material was complete and the data still did not support a named cause. The gaps that cannot be verified from exports are listed before you pay, not after.
  • The fee credited against the work that follows: fully toward a Launch Package, half toward the first month of a monthly system, if you move within two weeks of getting the report. Findings date quickly, which is why the window is short.

The refund and the credit are mutually exclusive. Apply the credit and the guarantee closes — you cannot have the money back and the money applied.

When the free one is the better choice

If you are shopping for a studio rather than for an answer, take the free audit from three of them and watch what each one notices first. That tells you who sees what you see. It is a reasonable way to choose a partner, and it costs you nothing but an hour.

Pay for a diagnosis when the problem has already cost you money and you need the cause named rather than described.

What it costs here

Visual review, $1,500. With a read of your own posting and ad numbers, $2,500. Three business days, counted from the point we have everything we need from you rather than from the day you pay. The ads version is $3,500 over five business days, and it is not a fit if your ads have been running less than two or three months — there is not enough history in the account to read.

Everything runs from exports and screenshots you send. No logins, no account access, nothing to hand over.
