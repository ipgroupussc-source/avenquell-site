Who we are

The ideas were never the problem.

I have always had more ideas than places to put them — campaigns, angles, ways to make a product finally look like what it actually is. For a long time the only thing I regretted was that I couldn't hand those ideas to the businesses that needed them: the ones doing real work, quietly, while louder and emptier things took the attention.

That is what Avenquell is for. Not to produce more content — to take some of the decisions off a founder's desk and hand back something they can act on. Here is what is broken. Here is why. Here is the order to fix it.

Why this work

Cheap tools were supposed to help. They filled the field with glitter.

Digital tools became cheap and available, and that was supposed to help. Instead it filled the field with people who look professional and aren't. For an owner who genuinely needs help, the glitter makes it impossible to tell what to look at — they can't judge the work, so they buy the louder pitch.

So we start by naming the problem in writing, before anyone spends another dollar. Every price is published. Nothing ships without a person reviewing it.

Charleston

A city that kept itself.

We work out of Charleston, South Carolina. It has grown, and it is still recognisably its own: its history intact, its character not flattened into another skyline. That is the same thing we try to do for a brand — growth that doesn't cost you your face.

The name

A vessel someone holds, and pours from.

Aven

From awen — the Welsh word for poetic inspiration, the muse of the bards. Underneath it lies an older sense: to breathe, to blow. Air. In the myth the inspiration is brewed in a cauldron, and it has to be ladled out — carried to someone before it is worth anything.

Quell

From the German Quelle: a spring, a source. It is also the English quell — to settle, to quiet.

Air and water, a cauldron and a spring — it is all one image: a vessel someone holds and pours from. That is the work. We carry what you need and set it down where you can use it, and quiet the glitter around it, so what matters is finally visible.
